Current setup

This app is configured for a zero-budget, Android-friendly workflow. It uses Google Sheets as a CRM layer, stores prospect data locally, and creates outreach drafts with clear human review and approval. It does not send messages automatically.

Meta Ads Library and public business pages are treated as research sources, not as proof that a business needs your design work. All claims must be public, observable, and verifiable or labeled as Unverified.

  • Keep CSV exports and audit records
