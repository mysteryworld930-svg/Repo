Current setup

This app is configured for a zero-budget, Android-friendly workflow. It uses Google Sheets as a local CRM layer, generates outreach drafts with Google AI Studio-style prompting patterns, and keeps a human approval gate before any message is sent.

External integrations are intentionally limited to honest, public-source workflows. Instagram, LinkedIn, and Meta automations are not enabled by default because they require official platform access, business approval, and compliance checks.
